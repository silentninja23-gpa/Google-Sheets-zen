🌿 Welcome to the Zen Sanctuary & 100 Cozy Mini-Games Hub!																									
																									
Step away from the noise and transform your ordinary Google Sheet into a peaceful, calming digital oasis. Designed entirely around relaxation, mindfulness, and gentle cozy vibes, this spreadsheet acts as your personal hub for unwinding, tracking your inner calm, and playing a massive collection of interactive mini-games right inside your cells.																									
																									
🌟 What Is This?																									
																									
This project is an interactive, script-powered relaxation suite built entirely inside Google Sheets. It features:																									
																									
The Main Oasis Dashboard: A live-updating visual sanctuary complete with mindfulness metrics, calm coin tracking, and a relaxing daily reflection log.																									
																									
100 Playable Cozy Mini-Games: A diverse library of puzzle, sensory, creative, focus, and idle mini-games that you can launch instantly.																									
																									
The Cozy Crossword Puzzle: A dedicated, interactive word puzzle built directly into your sheet with custom clues and an automated answer-checker.																									
🛠️ How It Works																									
1. The Interactive Top Menu (🌸 Zen Sanctuary)																									
																									
Once installed, a custom menu will appear at the top of your Google Sheet. This is your command center for everything:																									
																									
Dashboard & Reflection: Click 🍃 Open Main Garden Dashboard to view your live stats and visual sanctuary.																									
																									
The Crossword: Click ✏️ Open Cozy Crossword Puzzle to generate a custom word grid, then use ✅ Check My Crossword Answers to check your work and earn bonus Calm Coins!																									
																									
Mini-Game Launchers:																									
																									
Play a completely random activity using 🎲 Play Random Cozy Mini-Game.																									
																									
Type any number from 1 to 100 using 🔢 Play Mini-Game by Number to launch that specific game.																									
																									
Browse categorized genres (Puzzle, Sensory, Creative, Focus, Idle) directly from the submenus.																									
																									
Mindful Actions: Brew warm tea, practice deep breathing, trace a zen garden sand grid, or pull a daily affirmation to log positive reflections.
